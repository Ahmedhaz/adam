Questions

Before you start

Is it free?

Yes. Play it in your browser with no account, no ads and nothing to buy.

Can I play it on my phone?

Yes. Open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen: it then works like an app, even offline. An iPhone app is in beta.

Is it really in Arabic?

Fully. Every screen, dilemma, mentor note and playbook page is written in Arabic and English, and you can switch at any time without losing your game.

How long is a game?

A full run is about a year of game weeks, 30 to 60 minutes. Challenges are shorter. Your game saves automatically.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There's no account and nothing is sent to a server.

Can you build a company before the cash, or you, run out?

Most players don't make it on their first try. That's the point.
